Mandelbrot Deep Zoom
A GPU renderer that descends 33 decades into the Mandelbrot set, written in Rust and WGSL and running on both Apple Silicon and cloud NVIDIA from one source. Perturbation with exponential-map strips, so the plane is rendered once in log-polar form rather than once per frame. The result is Falling Into Scepter Valley: 5,510 frames, 3:04 at 4K30, ending on a 634 pixel minibrot.
